Questions

Before you book

Does every package include unlimited prints?

The listed instant-print packages state unlimited printing during the covered hours. Confirm your selected duration and print size before payment.

Is transport included?

Several packages mention free transport within 50 km. The venue must be checked because extra travel or access fees may apply.

Can customers pay by installment?

The vendor advertises Atome installment payments. Eligibility and service fees are subject to Atome and the active merchant checkout.

Can we use our own branding?

Yes. Custom photostrips are included in many packages, while straight or curved custom backdrops can be quoted separately.

Does this preview process real payments?

No. The checkout below demonstrates the proposed customer flow. Live FPX, DuitNow QR, card or Atome processing requires merchant credentials and a secure payment backend.
